1. Scope and core principles

This Privacy Policy explains how information is accessed, processed, stored, and shared when you use ReviKat, an Android application for Building Information Modeling (BIM), Computer-Aided Design (CAD), parametric family creation and editing, drawings, technical documentation, building systems, and other engineering-related functions.

ReviKat is designed to enable on-device processing of your projects. Accessing a file within the app does not, by itself, mean that Othala receives that file. This policy distinguishes between (a) information processed or stored on your device, (b) information transferred to other devices, applications, or services at your direction, and (c) information you provide to Othala or that is processed by an external provider.

You retain the ownership and other rights that apply to your projects and files. Othala does not gain intellectual-property ownership of your project contents simply because you use ReviKat. Othala will not use your projects to train its own or third-party artificial intelligence models without separate, specific authorization.

2. Information ReviKat may process

2.1 BIM/CAD projects and technical documents

Depending on the features you use, ReviKat may access, create, edit, display, import, export, or save:

  • Two- and three-dimensional geometry; architectural, structural, and mechanical, electrical, and plumbing (MEP) models; parametric families; materials; components; and associated properties.
  • Drawings, sheets, dimensions, schedules, tables, title blocks, views, levels, sections, annotations, and reference images.
  • Project metadata, including filenames, object identifiers, author information entered or embedded by a user, revisions, dates, construction-site details, and geographic references where present in a file.
  • Native ReviKat project files and external formats actually supported by the import and export tools in the version you install.

These files may contain business-confidential information, trade secrets, or personal information included by you or another project contributor. Local modeling operations do not require your project files to be sent to Othala.

2.2 Preferences and application state

ReviKat may store language, measurement units, interface settings, visualization preferences, recently used tools, editing state, document references, session recovery data, and other preferences on your device to keep the app configured and help you resume work.

2.3 Technical diagnostics (RDiag)

ReviKat includes technical diagnostic capabilities intended to help identify crashes, errors, and performance or stability problems. A diagnostic report may contain the app version, device model, Android version, CPU architecture, memory usage, thread counts, rendering times, frames-per-second measurements, executed operations, tool state, error messages, recent events, and information about a previous application exit.

A diagnostic report may also contain names, identifiers, or references associated with the project being edited. Diagnostic reports should therefore not automatically be regarded as anonymous. The existence of an on-device diagnostic log does not mean it has been transmitted to Othala. Before sending an exported report, review its contents and remove confidential information when appropriate.

If you voluntarily send a diagnostic report to Othala for support, Othala may use the information in that report to investigate the issue and improve reliability, subject to the retention and security practices described below.

2.4 In-app purchases

When in-app purchases are available and you buy features or add-ons through Google Play, Google processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. ReviKat may receive or query information necessary to recognize, validate, or restore purchased entitlements, such as a product identifier, purchase status, purchase token, and technical transaction details provided by Google Play.

Othala does not ask for your complete payment-card number or card security code inside ReviKat to process purchases made through Google Play. Google's own payment records are governed by Google's policies. This section applies to purchasing features only when they are enabled in the published app.

2.5 Support communications

If you voluntarily contact Othala, Othala may process your contact email address, message, attachments, and technical information you choose to submit in order to handle your request. Please do not attach complete projects, personal information, or confidential files unless they are genuinely necessary for support.

3. Why this information is used

Information available to ReviKat is used for purposes connected to the app's functions, including opening and editing projects; displaying geometry, drawings, and documentation; storing preferences and recovering sessions; importing and exporting files; performing technical calculations and checks; recognizing purchases; diagnosing errors; responding to support requests; and, if available and activated by you, supporting collaboration with other devices.

Othala will not use your project models, drawings, or documents for personalized advertising, commercial profiling, sale as a dataset, or training Othala's or a third party's AI models without separate, specific authorization.

4. Local storage, file access, and device backups

Information used for local work may be saved in ReviKat's private app storage or in a location you choose using Android's file-management features. If you export a document to a shared folder, external drive, or another application, that content also becomes subject to the controls and practices of the destination you select.

ReviKat may keep temporary files, checkpoints, recovery copies, and session information to help prevent loss of work. The availability, storage duration, and deletion of these files depend on the features actually implemented in the installed version.

Android backup notice: Backup, restore, or device-to-device transfer features provided by Android, Google, or your device manufacturer may operate independently of Othala. Their scope depends on system settings and the app's backup configuration. We do not state that all data stays exclusively on your device unless the release build's backup rules have been verified.

5. File sharing and local-network collaboration

You may choose to export or share files through other applications, services, or storage destinations. ReviKat facilitates the operation you request, but the provider or recipient you select may process that content under its own privacy terms.

If a version of ReviKat includes collaboration over a local area network (LAN) or Wi-Fi, this function may exchange connection and presence information (such as a session or device identifier and local network address), project revisions, changes, and portions or copies of a project necessary to synchronize work. Other authorized participants in that session may receive the shared information.

Local networking does not automatically mean end-to-end encryption. The actual authentication mechanisms, encryption controls, and scope of transferred information must be verified against the published version. Where local collaboration is available, use it only with trusted participants and networks appropriate for the sensitivity of your work.

6. External providers and recipients

Depending on which functions you use, the following parties may be involved:

  • Google Play: app distribution, updates, licensing, and in-app purchases.
  • Android, Google, and device manufacturers: operating-system file handling, backup, restoration, and device-transfer services, where enabled.
  • Applications, cloud services, or recipients selected by you: files and other content you explicitly export or share.
  • Participants in an enabled local collaboration session: the session and project information you choose to synchronize.
  • Email or support providers: communications you voluntarily send to Othala.

Othala does not sell BIM/CAD project files, drawings, diagnostic reports, or users' personal information.

7. Android permissions and device controls

ReviKat may need access to files you select and may use Android capabilities necessary for importing, exporting, and sharing documents. If an installed version includes LAN connectivity or other functions requiring additional permissions, Android may request the applicable authorization.

This document does not assume that ReviKat accesses your camera, microphone, precise location, contacts, or the full contents of your device storage. The permissions actually requested must match the published app manifest and the behavior of all included third-party libraries. Any additional sensitive access must be reviewed and disclosed before release.

8. Security and confidentiality

Othala intends to limit information handling to what is necessary for the functions provided and to apply safeguards appropriate to the risks involved. The actual level of protection depends on the published app version, device configuration, and any destination or connection you choose for sharing information.

You should protect your device using Android security features, control who receives your files, and avoid sharing diagnostic reports that disclose confidential project details. Neither local storage nor membership in a private network guarantees absolute confidentiality. Specific claims about encryption, recovery, and backup protections will be made only after they have been verified for the production release.

9. Data retention and deletion

  • Locally saved projects and documents: remain in their storage location until you delete, replace, or manage them. Uninstalling ReviKat generally removes the app's private data, but may not delete documents exported elsewhere or backup copies created by the operating system or third parties.
  • Preferences, temporary files, and recovery data: may remain while the app is installed or until a cleanup or rotation mechanism removes them.
  • On-device RDiag records: their storage location, retention period, and deletion procedures depend on the actual release implementation. Reports voluntarily sent to Othala are treated as support communications.
  • Purchases: entitlement-related information may be retained as needed to verify and restore purchases. Google independently determines the retention of its payment records.
  • Support requests: correspondence may be retained for the time needed to address the request and meet applicable legal obligations. [BEFORE PUBLICATION: SPECIFY THE ACTUAL RETENTION PERIOD OR OBJECTIVE RETENTION CRITERIA AND THE DELETION PROCEDURE FOR SUPPORT EMAILS AND ATTACHMENTS.]

To ask Othala to delete personal information that Othala has received, contact the address listed below. Othala cannot directly delete files that exist only on your device, in external services you selected, or in Google's independently administered systems.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on the laws that apply to you, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, object to, or otherwise control the processing of your personal information. In Mexico, these may include the rights of access, rectification, cancellation, and opposition (commonly referred to as ARCO rights).

Send a request to [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL], describing the right you want to exercise, the information or processing concerned, and a way for Othala to reply. We may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify your identity or authorization to act for another person. Requests will be handled according to applicable legal requirements and deadlines. Certain records may need to be retained where lawfully required.

You can also limit optional disclosures by not sharing files or diagnostic reports, withdrawing device permissions where Android allows, and using available local deletion options.

11. Children and intended audience

ReviKat is primarily designed for architecture, engineering, design, technical documentation, and related learning activities. It is not specifically directed at children. The audience designation in Google Play and any safeguards required for minors must match the actual published version and applicable laws.

12. New features and changes to this policy

Future features such as online account creation, internet-based synchronization, cloud project hosting, usage analytics, external AI services, or new purchase mechanisms are not automatically covered or authorized by this document. Before such features are introduced, Othala must review what data they process, their purposes, recipients, technical safeguards, and any legally required notices or consent mechanisms.

If ReviKat later offers user accounts created within the app, the relevant account and associated-data deletion procedures will also be provided as required by the platform and applicable law.

Othala may update this policy to reflect functional, technical, or legal changes. An updated version will display its revision date and will be made available inside ReviKat and through the public privacy-policy link associated with its Google Play listing.
